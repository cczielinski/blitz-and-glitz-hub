What to say

Scripts

Door prize / raffle ask

Use this for a quick, low-pressure ask — texting or messaging a local business about donating an item, not a dollar sponsorship.

Hi! 😊 I'm helping organize Blitz & Glitz, our Gunter Tiger Football Booster Club's annual ladies' night fundraiser, and I wanted to see if your business might be interested in donating a door prize or raffle item. This is our third year, and it's become such a fun community event! We'll have around 125–150 local women from Gunter and the surrounding area, and if there's one thing our community does well, it's supporting local businesses. We truly love shopping local and supporting the businesses that give back to our community. Gift cards, products, services, or anything you'd be willing to donate would be so appreciated! We'll recognize all of our donors at the event and on social media as a thank-you. If you'd like to help, I'd be happy to pick up the donation whenever it's convenient for you. Thank you so much for considering supporting our Gunter Tiger football program—we truly appreciate it! 🐅💙
Sponsorship ask

Use this when pitching a dollar-level sponsorship ($100–$5,000) rather than an in-kind donation — it leads with the program's size and reach.

Hi! 😊 I'm reaching out on behalf of Blitz & Glitz, the Gunter Tiger Football Booster Club's annual ladies' night fundraiser, to see if [Business Name] would be interested in becoming a sponsor this year. Gunter Tiger Football is actually the largest program at Gunter High School — 155 of our roughly 390 students take part. That means a Blitz & Glitz sponsorship puts your name in front of parents, extended family, and the whole community that turns out on game nights — triple the exposure of a typical local sponsorship! Sponsorship levels range from $100 to $5,000, with perks like website and social media recognition, event signage, tickets to Blitz & Glitz, and a spot in our sponsor Playbook — every level directly supports our Gunter Tiger athletes. I'd love to send over our full sponsorship details and answer any questions — would you be open to a quick conversation this week? Thank you so much for considering supporting our Gunter Tiger football program — we truly appreciate it! 🐅💙
