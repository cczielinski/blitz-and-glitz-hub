Calendar

Committee meetings and working sessions. Tap Add to Calendar on any event to save it — it works on both iPhone and Android.

📅 How it works: each "Add to Calendar" button downloads a standard calendar file (.ics). Open the download on an iPhone and it offers to add the event straight into Calendar; on Android it opens through Google Calendar (or whichever calendar app is set as default). No app or account needed on our end.
Need a meeting added? Just tell Claude the date, time, and location (e.g. “add a committee meeting Aug 12 at 6pm at the school library”) and it'll show up here for everyone.
